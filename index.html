Maximum-resolution thumbnails provide a larger image when this size is available. They are useful for examining visual details, studying thumbnail layouts, and keeping high-quality reference images.

Standard Definition — 640 × 480 Pixels

Standard-definition thumbnails can be suitable for general viewing and reference purposes. The actual dimensions depend on the image available for the particular video.

High Quality — 480 × 360 Pixels

High-quality thumbnails at this resolution can be useful for smaller previews and basic reference tasks when the image is available.

Medium and Default Resolutions

Medium and default thumbnail images may have different dimensions. They can be useful when larger versions are unavailable or when a smaller preview is sufficient.

Why Is a YouTube Thumbnail Not Showing?

Sometimes a thumbnail cannot be retrieved or displayed. The problem may be related to the submitted link, the availability of the image, network restrictions, or the browser.

1. Check the video URL

Make sure you copied the correct link and that it points to a YouTube video. If the link contains a typing error, copy it again from YouTube.

2. Try another available resolution

Not every video has an accessible image at every resolution. If a larger thumbnail fails to load, check whether another available size works.

3. Check your internet connection

Thumbnail previews require the relevant image to load. A slow connection, browser extension, or network restriction may prevent an image from appearing correctly.

4. Try a different browser

If the problem continues, try opening the page in another up-to-date browser. This can help determine whether the issue is specific to your current browser or its settings.

Using Thumbnails for Content Research

Thumbnail images can help creators understand how videos are presented to viewers. When reviewing examples, pay attention to the main subject, contrast, composition, text readability, and the relationship between the image and the video's topic.

For example, a gaming creator might compare how different channels position characters, display game environments, or use contrasting backgrounds. These observations can help inspire an original design rather than copying another creator's finished thumbnail.

A downloaded thumbnail does not reveal how well the original video performs. Views and other performance metrics depend on many factors, including the topic, audience, title, distribution, and viewer behavior.

Can You Reuse a Downloaded YouTube Thumbnail?

Being able to download an image does not automatically grant permission to republish or modify it. A thumbnail may contain artwork, photographs, logos, or other material protected by copyright or other rights.

If you plan to publish a downloaded thumbnail, check whether you have permission or another valid legal basis to use it. For your own videos, keep copies of your original thumbnail files so you can reuse or edit your designs when needed.
